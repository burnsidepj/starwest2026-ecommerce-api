Median versus tail, per endpoint

Each row spans that endpoint's p50 to its p95. A long span means most requests are fast while a minority are far slower — the signature of requests queueing behind blocked work, not of a slow endpoint. Healthcheck and checkout do almost no work of their own, yet their spans are the widest in relative terms.
